Out of the box: M5Stack factory firmware

These features belong to the original factory application. We replaced that application with community development firmware; they are not automatically active in the current research MOD.

AI Agent

Wake phrase “Hi, StackChan”, spoken question answering, configurable model, voice, personality and memory.

Expressive companion

Animated expressions, idle movement, touch and shake reactions.

App interaction

Video viewing, remote avatar control and camera/servo control; factory guide says avatar voice calls are not yet available.

Control and updates

ESP-NOW remote control, dance routines, online app downloads and OTA updates.

The factory AI Agent’s question answering is not evidence of an LLM hosted inside the robot. Service entitlement, pricing and reuse from custom firmware are still unverified.

Reusable capabilities in our firmware

Available interfaces and implementations; not a claim that every provider is configured or recently hardware-tested.

Audio

Microphone recording/playback; OpenAI Realtime and Gemini Live conversation workers; remote Whisper STT and several TTS adapters.

Offline Japanese voice

stackchan-voice synthesises Japanese on CoreS3. Our English research sentence is a bundled recording, not general English TTS.

Hardware interfaces

Face/display, bounded servo motion, camera, touch, LEDs and networking. Available APIs do not mean every hardware feature has been validated in our deployment.

Specification draft

Voice-controlled Pomodoro

“Hi Joy” then “Pomodoro”: 20 minutes focus, 5 minutes rest, then finish. Voice pause/resume/cancel; unobtrusive countdown; gentle phase chimes. Research speech waits until finish or cancellation. Reboot returns to normal mode.

Current operating boundaries

Research operation needs power, Wi-Fi and an awake, reachable Mac. USB is for deployment and diagnostics. Local camera images remain on the robot, and detection locates faces without identifying people. Face alignment depends on position and lighting. Status transport is bearer-authenticated HTTP on a trusted LAN, without encryption. A saved-note gate checks structure and provenance, not factual accuracy.
