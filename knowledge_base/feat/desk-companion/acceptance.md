# Acceptance

Automated checks cover idle reset, repeated intervals, duplicate triggers, foreground suppression, pause/resume, interruption cleanup and motor failure cleanup.

Pending hardware: drawer visibility, small movement geometry, pause/research/Pomodoro handover, no motion during paused Pomodoro, a real 15-minute idle interval and torque release. Automated tests do not certify physical arrival or torque state.

Native Piu simulator checks cover section routing, settings/choice retention during timer changes, switch knob position, countdown text updates retaining the same touch target, and rendering at host display-list capacity. Updated sweep checks require four turns in the 30–45 degree upward range, at least two seconds per turn, and neutral cleanup.

Coexistence test verifies detection during automatic movement, no cancellation by an isolated stroke, immediate happy feedback before motor handover, neutral cleanup before petting motion, and the next automatic sweep after 15 quiet minutes.
